Everything We Provide

Engineered to Handle Every Enterprise Payroll Complexity

From complex academic pay matrix formulas to statutory reporting and retrospective arrears, Virtoy Payroll covers the complete compensation lifecycle.

7th CPC Pay Matrix Engine

Automated support for Central Pay Commission Academic Pay Levels (10 to 14A) and Administrative Levels (1 to 13) with automated annual increments (July / January).

  • Academic Levels 10, 11, 12, 13A, 14, 14A
  • Administrative Levels 1 through 13
  • Dynamic Dearness Allowance (DA) Indexing
  • City Classification HRA Rates (X 27%, Y 18%, Z 9%)

Intelligent Tax & TDS Forecaster

Automated comparison between New Tax Regime and Old Tax Regime with live monthly TDS forecasts for employees, avoiding year-end tax shocks.

  • Section 80C, 80D, 24(b) deduction tracking
  • Online employee IT declaration & proof upload
  • Year-To-Date (YTD) salary tax statement
  • Auto-switch to most beneficial tax regime

1-Click Payslips & Form 16 Engine

Generate institute-branded PDF payslips with security barcodes, granular earnings/deductions breakdown, and automated Form 16 Part A & B downloads.

  • Bulk & individual PDF payslip downloads
  • Form 16 (Part A & Part B) generation
  • Automated password encryption (PAN + DOB)
  • Direct email dispatch to registered faculty

Retrospective Arrears Calculator

Eliminate manual spreadsheet headaches. Calculate Dearness Allowance (DA) arrears, promotion backpay, and pay revisions across multiple months in seconds.

  • Multi-month retroactive difference computation
  • Sanction order & approval sheet generation
  • Automated NPS/TDS adjustments on arrears
  • 1-Click integration into active payroll run

Direct Bank Payout & Disbursal

Instant export of formatted bank direct credit files (NEFT, RTGS, NACH) compliant with major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB, Canara).

  • Bank Advice & Payment Register generation
  • Corporate Net Banking batch upload files
  • Automated account number & IFSC validation
  • Rejection reconciliation tracking

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict separation of duties between Finance Officers, Super Admins, Department Approvers, and Employees with immutable audit logging.

  • Finance Operator & Finance Admin roles
  • Registrar / Director approving authority
  • Self-Service Employee Portal (ESS)
  • Full timestamped audit trails of all modifications

Statutory & Pension Compliances

Complete built-in compliance with Central Govt norms for National Pension System (NPS), Central Govt Health Scheme (CGHS), and Professional Tax.

  • NPS Tier-1 (10% Employee + 14% Govt Share)
  • NSDL / CRA File Export for PRAN uploads
  • State-wise Professional Tax (PT) slabs
  • Gratuity & Leave Encashment calculations

Employee Self-Service (ESS)

Empower faculty and staff with a modern web portal to view salary slips, track tax deductions, submit investment proofs, and download annual certificates.

  • Mobile-optimized self-service login
  • Interactive historical payslip archive
  • Tax simulator & deduction calculator
  • Automated notification & SMS/Email alerts
